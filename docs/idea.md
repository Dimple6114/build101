# The idea

## Problem

Small business owners who sell through Instagram do the work twice: the real work (restoring a piece of furniture, making a bag) and then all of the social media around it: photos, captions, posting every day, answering comments and DMs, closing sales. The product is good; the owner just can't be a full-time maker and a full-time community manager at once.

## Target user

Any non-technical entrepreneur who sells through Instagram and lives in WhatsApp. First pilot: **Katei** (furniture restoration, Uruguay).

## Value proposition

An AI **community manager** the owner talks to on WhatsApp. The owner only does their work, takes photos and sends them (plus a voice note if they want). Behind the chat, a team of AI agents does everything else:

| Agent | Job |
|---|---|
| Community manager (orchestrator) | Talks to the owner on WhatsApp, routes work, asks for approval |
| Strategist / virality analyst | Weekly plan, special dates (Valentine's, Mother's Day, Christmas, Black Friday), what to post when |
| Copywriter | Captions and hashtags in the business's own voice |
| Graphic designer | Turns real photos into carousels and before/after layouts |
| Reels editor | Short videos from the photos |
| Sales agent | Answers comments and DMs, closes sales, notifies the owner |
| Metrics analyst | Reports what worked and feeds it back into the plan |

The system learns how the business talks (brand voice) from its past posts and from the owner's corrections.

## Not this (for now)

- Back-office operations (suppliers, staff, orders). It may come later. See [decisions/0001](decisions/0001-focus-marketing.md).

## Business model (draft)

Tiered monthly subscription, starting at a low entry price and upgrading as results show. See [roadmap.md](roadmap.md#pricing-draft).
