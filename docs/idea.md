# The idea

## Problem

Small business owners run operations through chat, notes and memory. Delegating, following up and keeping track eats their day, and existing tools assume they will learn a dashboard.

## Target user (draft)

A non-technical owner of a small business — e.g. a 50–60-year-old woman running a furniture business. Lives in WhatsApp, not in SaaS dashboards.

## Value proposition (draft)

One AI "chief of staff" the **owner** talks to in a familiar chat interface. It runs or delegates day-to-day operations (follow-ups, reminders, orders, coordination with staff and suppliers) and asks for approval before acting.

## Not this (for now)

- A customer-facing sales/support bot — that space is crowded (see [competitors.md](competitors.md)).
