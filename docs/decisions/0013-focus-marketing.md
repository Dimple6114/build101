# 0013: Focus the MVP on marketing and social media

- Date: 2026-10-17
- Status: proposed

## Context

The first draft was an owner-facing operations assistant (follow-ups, suppliers, staff). Talking to real owners (Katei, a bag seller), the pain that repeats is social media: the owner already does it, but it doubles their workload.

## Decision

The MVP is an AI community manager: content from the owner's photos, Instagram publishing, replies to comments and DMs, sales, weekly plan and metrics. Operations are out of scope for now.

## Consequences

- The product is now partly customer-facing (comments, DMs), a crowded space; our difference is that the owner runs everything from WhatsApp and does nothing else.
- Instagram is the first and main network.
